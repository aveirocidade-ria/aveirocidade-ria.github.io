A PERSONAL APPEAL FOR THE RIA

The Ria is alive
Let's keep it that way

I come to the Ria to watch the birds, hear the wind through the reeds and look out over the water. But even here, the roar of the A25 is hard to escape. And nearby, at Antiga Lota, there are plans for homes, a hotel and shops. What happens to this living landscape if we keep treating it as space to build on?

I don't want to just complain and move on. I want answers — and a real chance to protect this place. On 8 November I wrote to Portugal's Environment Minister and asked Parliament how I can start a petition. The petition is still being prepared; there is no signature link yet.

Read my letter (English translation)
Dear Minister of Environment and Energy,
Professor Maria da Graça Carvalho,

I am writing as a resident of the municipality of Aveiro, concerned about the effective protection of the Ria de Aveiro. For me, the Ria is not merely a tourist image or a landscape of which the region is proud. It is a living place that deserves protection through concrete decisions.

On 18 March 2026, Ordinance No. 115/2026/1 approved the management plan for the Ria de Aveiro Special Area of Conservation, the Rio Vouga Special Area of Conservation and the Ria de Aveiro Special Protection Area. On 17 June, Council of Ministers Resolution No. 125/2026 revised the National Strategy for Nature Conservation and Biodiversity 2030. I welcome these commitments, but would like to know how they are reflected on the ground, particularly near the city of Aveiro.

Two situations concern me and should be considered together, without confusing the responsibilities of the different authorities:

1. The A25 near the salt pans and channels. During my visits, road traffic noise is persistent. I would like to know what assessments exist regarding noise, lighting, drainage, the quality and treatment of road runoff, and possible effects on birds and habitats. What measures have been implemented, what results have they achieved, and what gaps warrant new assessments or mitigation measures?

2. Antiga Lota / Parque das Marinhas. The municipality is developing an urban project including housing, a hotel and commercial spaces beside the lagoon landscape. I ask for clarification of the relevant environmental studies and how an alternative based on conservation and ecological restoration, without new buildings, could be studied and publicly discussed before irreversible decisions are made.

I am not claiming that pollution from the road has been proven, that no protective measures exist, or that all of Antiga Lota lies within Natura 2000. Precisely for this reason, I request documented information and an analysis taking into account both existing pressures and planned interventions.

I therefore ask:

a) That the Ministry promote coordination between ICNF, APA and, within their respective remits, the authorities responsible for road infrastructure and municipal planning;
b) That the existing studies, measures already implemented and relevant monitoring findings for these areas be identified;
c) That it be clarified whether an integrated assessment of pressures and cumulative impacts on the Ria's habitats is planned and, if necessary, additional protective measures;
d) That an alternative involving conservation and ecological restoration of Antiga Lota, including its possible benefits and limitations, be considered and publicly discussed;
e) That I receive a written reply identifying the responsible authorities, next steps and, where available, indicative time frames.

This is not merely a municipal matter. The management plan approved in 2026 covers areas in twelve municipalities, and conserving the Ria is part of national and European commitments.

WHAT I HOPE WE CAN DO · 02

I'd like more people to have a say

One person writing letters is a start. But the Ria does not belong to us. It is a living world in its own right. Birds, plants and countless other creatures depend on it. We humans have a powerful influence on this place — and a responsibility to protect it, not destroy it. I'm preparing a petition to ask Parliament to look closely at the effects of the A25, share what is known and give nature a real chance at Antiga Lota.

Read the full petition draft (English translation)
To the President of the Assembly of the Republic,

Subject: Effective protection of the Ria de Aveiro — from promises to decisions

The Ria de Aveiro is a living landscape of major ecological and historical importance, connected to communities and municipalities across Portugal's Central Region. Its beauty is celebrated in images and speeches about Aveiro and Portugal. But recognising its value also requires concrete conservation decisions.

On 18 March 2026, Ordinance No. 115/2026/1 approved the management plan for the Ria de Aveiro and Rio Vouga Special Areas of Conservation and the Ria de Aveiro Special Protection Area. On 17 June 2026, Council of Ministers Resolution No. 125/2026 revised the National Strategy for Nature Conservation and Biodiversity 2030.

This petition seeks to ensure that these commitments are accompanied by public information, appropriate assessment of existing pressures, and consideration of alternatives that protect nature.

Two local issues illustrate the need for a combined perspective:

— The A25 crosses or runs close to the lagoon landscape, including salt pans and channels. Official road-noise documents exist, but it remains important to clarify what is known in the most sensitive areas about noise, lighting, drainage, road runoff quality and potential ecological effects, as well as existing measures and their effectiveness.

— At Antiga Lota / Parque das Marinhas, the municipality of Aveiro is planning an urban development with housing, a hotel and commercial spaces. Residents should be able to see the relevant studies, and the option of conserving and ecologically restoring the site without new buildings should be seriously studied and debated as an alternative.

This petition does not claim proven environmental offences or pollution, nor that all of Antiga Lota belongs to Natura 2000. It seeks clarification, prevention, scientific assessment and transparency.

The Assembly of the Republic is therefore asked, within its parliamentary oversight and recommendation powers, to:

1. Request public information from the Government on the implementation, indicators, conservation measures and monitoring of the management plan approved by Ordinance No. 115/2026/1.

2. Through the relevant parliamentary committee, seek clarification from the appropriate ministries and bodies, including the environment and infrastructure sectors, ICNF and APA, concerning habitat protection near the A25 and the potential need for further assessment or mitigation.

3. Recommend that the cumulative effects of existing infrastructure and planned urban changes on the Ria's landscape and habitats be considered in an integrated and transparent manner.

4. Encourage public clarification of the studies available on Antiga Lota and, while respecting municipal powers, a public comparison of the development project with an alternative based on conservation and ecological restoration without new construction.

5. Make public the responses from the competent authorities and any proposed or adopted measures.

The Ria is not an empty landscape awaiting development. It provides habitat for birds, fish, plants and many other species. Its value does not depend on generating profit or attracting tourists.

Protecting the Ria must mean more than promoting it in documents. It must mean caring for it through decisions.

Respectfully submitted,

[Name of the first petitioner — to be completed after eligibility is confirmed]
